# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook (sample document for evaluation)

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and
can be requested through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Leave carry-over
Unused annual leave carries over to the next year up to a maximum of 5 days. Any balance above 5 days expires
on 31 March. Carry-over days cannot be encashed unless the employee leaves the company.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than
two consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Remote work
The company follows a hybrid model. Employees may work remotely three days a week, and are expected in the
office on Tuesdays and Thursdays for team collaboration. Fully remote arrangements need director approval.

## Home internet reimbursement
Remote workers are reimbursed up to 1,000 rupees per month for home internet on submission of the bill.

## Notice period
The standard notice period for resignation is 60 days. During probation, which lasts 3 months, the notice
period is 15 days. The company may waive part of the notice period at its discretion.

## Learning and development
Each employee has a learning budget of 40,000 rupees per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused
budget does not roll over. Manager approval is needed for any single purchase above 10,000 rupees.

## Equipment
Laptops are refreshed every 36 months. Broken equipment is replaced within five working days. Employees must
return all equipment on their last working day.

## On-call
Engineers on the on-call rota receive a stipend of 1,500 rupees per week. The on-call engineer must respond
to a page within 15 minutes and may take a compensatory day off after a night incident.

## Security incidents
Any suspected security incident, such as a lost laptop or a phishing click, must be reported within 1 hour
to security@northwind.example. Do not attempt to investigate on your own or delete evidence.

## Travel and meals
While travelling for work, employees may claim a meal allowance of 800 rupees per day. Flights must be booked
through the company travel desk, and economy class is the default for trips under six hours.

## Code of conduct
Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised confidentially with HR or through the
anonymous ethics line. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.
